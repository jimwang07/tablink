Tablink is a bill-splitting app: scan receipts → share a link → friends claim items → settle up.

**Why:** Simplifies group expense splitting by automating OCR itemization and providing a no-account-needed guest claim flow.

**How to apply:** All work in this repo serves the scan → share → settle flow. Mobile app is the primary surface; guest-web is the lightweight claim experience for link recipients.
